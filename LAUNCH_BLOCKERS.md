with what Akash needs to supply. Checked against the live database on 2 Oct 2026.

**How to clear an item:** most are done in the admin (`/admin`). Upload the real image or video
link, untick **Sample**, publish. Items marked *(code)* need a one-line change from Claude Code.
Anything you don't have yet can be **unpublished** instead: sections with nothing published hide
themselves (FAQs fall back to the drafts until real ones are published).
